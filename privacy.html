Pegasus staff app: privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

This app is the staff rota app for Pegasus Adventure Breaks Ltd ("Pegasus", "we", "us"), 7 Shorehaven, Portsmouth PO6 4RU, company number 16640022. It is only for people who work for Pegasus.

  • Pegasus is the data controller. We decide what information the app holds and why.
  • Alex Rapier (DevonGrowth) is our data processor. They built the app and look after it for us, and only use the information to run and fix the app on our instructions.

If you have a question about your information, contact us at alex.rapier13@gmail.com.

What information the app holds

About you, if you work for us:

  • your name and mobile number (your mobile number is how you sign in)
  • your emergency contact (the name and number you give us)
  • whether you are approved to drive young people and whether you have your own vehicle for commuting
  • the days you say you are available, and any note you add
  • the rota: the days and camps you are on, part shifts, duty manager shifts and any notes about a shift
  • the mileage and expenses you claim, and whether they were approved or rejected (with the reason)
  • your pay: your day rate, what you are owed and what you have been paid (you can see your own pay in the app; only Pegasus's managers see everyone's)
  • notes Pegasus's managers write about your work (only managers see these)
  • a code that lets us send notifications to your phone, and the phone type (iPhone or Android)

About the young people we look after (entered by Pegasus's managers, never by the young people themselves): name, date of birth, which camp they are at, key information staff need to care for them safely (which may include health, behaviour or safeguarding information), the camp phone number, their carer's name and number, daily plans and notes. Young people do not use the app.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera or photos. It has no adverts and no tracking, and we never sell your information.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

What Why Lawful basis (UK GDPR)
Name, mobile, sign-in To let you sign in and to know who you are Contract (your work with us)
Availability, rota, duty manager shifts, notes To plan who works where and run each day safely Contract; legitimate interests (running the business safely)
Emergency contact To contact someone if you are hurt or unwell at work Legitimate interests; vital interests in an emergency
Driving and vehicle To plan transport and check who can drive young people Contract; legal obligation (safeguarding and insurance)
Mileage, expenses, pay To pay you correctly and keep the records the law requires Contract; legal obligation (tax and employment records)
Managers' notes about staff To manage and support staff Legitimate interests
Notification code To send you rota reminders and messages on your phone Legitimate interests
Young people's details To care for them safely and meet our duties as a care provider Legal obligation or public task under our placement contracts; for health or safeguarding information, UK GDPR Article 9(2)(h) and the Data Protection Act 2018 Schedule 1 conditions

Who can see it

  • Pegasus's managers (owners) can see everything in the app.
  • Staff see their own details, the rota for the days they need, the duty manager notes meant for them, and the young people's cards they need to do their job. Staff see their own pay, but never anyone else's pay, other people's claims, or managers' notes about staff.
  • Our developer (DevonGrowth) can access the data only to run, support and fix the app.

Where it is stored, and the companies that help us

Company What they do Where
Supabase Stores the app's data and handles signing in Amazon Web Services, London (eu-west-2)
Twilio Sends sign-in codes by text message, when text codes are switched on (they only get your mobile number and the code) United States (UK International Data Transfer Addendum)
Expo (650 Industries) Delivers app updates and passes notifications to Apple and Google United States (UK International Data Transfer Addendum)
Apple and Google Deliver notifications to your phone Their own services

Each of these companies only processes the information to provide their service to us, under a contract that requires them to keep it safe. Notifications never include young people's details or pay, only short messages such as "Critical information · New for Tue 3 Nov".

All information is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Data in the database is encrypted at rest. Your sign-in on your phone is stored encrypted.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in: until you sign out (the code for that phone) or delete your account (everything about your sign-in).
  • Pay, mileage and expenses: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as HMRC requires.
  • Rota, availability and notes: 6 years after you stop working for us.
  • Your name, mobile and emergency contact: while you work for us; when you leave, your mobile number and emergency contact are removed and the rest is kept for the periods above.
  • Young people's records: in line with Pegasus's records policy and the placing authority's requirements.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected, to have it deleted or restricted, to object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. Some information we must keep by law (for example pay records), so we may not be able to delete all of it. Ask at alex.rapier13@gmail.com; we will answer within one month.

You can correct your name, emergency contact and transport answers yourself on My profile in the app.

If you are unhappy with how we use your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

Deleting your account

Open the app, go to My profile, tap Delete my account, and confirm. This straight away:

  • deletes your sign-in, so you can't sign in to that account again
  • removes your mobile number, your emergency contact and your phone's notification code

Your name and work records (the days you worked, your availability, claims and pay) stay with Pegasus for the periods above, because we need them for pay and legal records. To ask for those to be removed too, email alex.rapier13@gmail.com.

If you can't open the app, see https://rapier97.github.io/pegasus-legal/delete-account.html or email us and we will delete your account for you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and tell staff in the app or by message.
