Delete your Pegasus account

Pegasus staff app, by Pegasus Adventure Breaks Ltd

In the app

  1. Open the Pegasus app and sign in.
  2. Tap My profile at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account, read what happens, then tap Delete my account again.

You're signed out straight away.

Without the app

Email alex.rapier13@gmail.com from anywhere, with your name and the mobile number you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days and email you when it's done. We may contact you on that number first to check it's you.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (you can't sign in to this account again)
  • Your mobile number
  • Your emergency contact
  • Your phone's notification code

What is kept, and for how long

Pegasus keeps your name and your work records (the days you worked, your availability, mileage and expense claims and pay) because we need them for pay and the law: pay, mileage and expenses for 6 years after the end of the tax year, and other work records for 6 years after you stop working for us. To ask for these to be removed sooner, email alex.rapier13@gmail.com; we will remove anything the law doesn't require us to keep.

If you come back to work for Pegasus, create a new account in the app with your number.

Full privacy policy: https://rapier97.github.io/pegasus-legal/privacy.html
